What Is KleptoFit?

Provides quality and exclusive fitness equipment that often suits in the interest of numerous item collectors, sport enthusiasts, athletes and even fitness influencers. Each item brings a challenge of their own. Our intention is to spread those durable products in popularity while maintaining a great user shipping experience.

Our Mission

Our goal is to spread our products' popularity to become known worldwide. So far, you can find our products in several fitness centers, and we are more than happy to collaborate with many more. KleptoFit is all about showcasing the potential of new toys to the fitness scene to part away with traditional barbell gym equipment standards.

Our Target Audience

If fitness was already a passion of yours, KleptoFit might be your piece of cake as we focus on providing equipment serving from moderate to elite fitness levels. Although our website is targeting mostly people who train, other people could pique interest too. For instance, collectors like to gather the most exclusive items they can put their hands on, influencers love challenging people with the most absurd toys on the web and friend groups often like to compete in specialized strength aspects. For KleptoFit, everybody counts.

Behind The Name

KleptoFit is a combination of two words, "Klepto" and "Fit". "Klepto" defines the desire to steal while "Fit" refers to fitness. Essentially, in our context the combination is used to show the delicasy of our products to a level where anyone would want to grab their hands on. You can compare our fitness products like a box of toys for a child, except here we're talking on the fitness level.
